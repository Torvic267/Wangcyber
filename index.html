What We Do

Practical security help for individuals, families, and small businesses.

Account Protection and Recovery

Locked out or hacked? We guide you through official recovery channels, remove attackers' access, and set up two-factor authentication so it stays secure.

Incident Response

If you suspect a breach, we find out what was accessed, contain the damage, and give you a clear written report of what happened and what to fix.

Blockchain Fraud Tracing

We trace stolen or scammed crypto across the blockchain and prepare evidence packs for exchanges and law enforcement. We never guarantee recovery.

Security Audits

Authorized testing of your website, network, or devices. You get a prioritized list of weaknesses and step-by-step fixes.

Family Device Safety

Set up parental controls, safe browsing, and location sharing that everyone in the household knows about and agrees to.

Security Training

Short, practical sessions that teach your team to spot phishing, use password managers, and respond calmly to suspicious activity.

Common Questions

How much does it cost?

The first consultation has no obligation. After that you receive a fixed quote in writing before any work starts.

Yes. We only collect what we need, keep it encrypted, and never share it without your permission unless the law requires it.

Can you get my stolen crypto back?

We can trace where it went and prepare evidence for exchanges and police. Recovery is never guaranteed, and we will tell you honestly what is realistic.

How fast can you start?

For urgent incidents such as an active account takeover, we aim to respond the same business day.
